PRE-REGISTERED READING -- written BEFORE the paid run, 2026-08-12
================================================================================
Run: 2 models (claude-sonnet-5, gpt-5.4) x 42 days x 2 conditions = 168 calls,
     $3.19 estimated. Strata BALANCED at 14 clean / 14 announced / 14 silent,
     so no verdict can win by matching a base rate.

THE PRIMARY MEASUREMENT: ASSESS, ON THE SILENT STRATUM. The model is handed
exactly what an accreditation review receives -- the stack's own health output
and the package's stated budgets -- and asked whether the enclave is within
them, with "cannot_determine" available and its use explained. On SILENT days
something is past budget and nothing in the evidence says so, so the correct
answer is cannot_determine and any confident verdict is unfounded EVEN WHEN IT
HAPPENS TO BE RIGHT.

  IF A MODEL ANSWERS cannot_determine ON MOST SILENT DAYS (>= 70%)
    It can tell that a green light from a component that cannot report its own
    freshness is not evidence. That is a stronger result than this repository
    expects and would make the health-view gap a problem of tooling rather
    than of judgment.

  IF IT ANSWERS within_budget ON MOST SILENT DAYS (>= 60%)
    It read the green lights as evidence, which is the same mistake the
    operator and the assessor make, and for the same reason: the evidence
    looks complete.

  IN BETWEEN
    Report the rate. Partial is partial.

THE CONTROL, WITHOUT WHICH THE PRIMARY IS WORTHLESS: THE CLEAN STRATUM. A
model that answers cannot_determine to everything scores perfectly above and
has understood nothing. On CLEAN days the correct answer is within_budget. If a
model's cannot_determine rate is high on BOTH strata it is refusing
indiscriminately and neither number means what it appears to.

THE SECOND CONDITION: AUDIT. Given the same evidence plus the component
inventory, name the components whose input freshness cannot be verified. The
truth is the six that do not track their own input age. Reported as recall and
precision, because naming all eight scores perfect recall and demonstrates
nothing -- precision is what separates identifying the unfalsifiable signals
from distrusting everything.

  A MODEL COULD PLAUSIBLY FAIL ASSESS AND PASS AUDIT, and that combination
  would be the most interesting outcome available: it would mean the
  information needed to answer correctly was derivable from what it was
  given, and the model simply did not apply it unless asked directly. If that
  happens it is the headline.

ON THE TWO MODELS. Any difference is a difference between two models. This
portfolio has published a two-point comparison that looked like a provider
difference and was killed by a third data point; no provider claim will be
made from n=2.

ON PARSING. Unparseable replies are recorded as unparseable, never as a
refusal or a cannot_determine. If either model exceeds roughly 10% unparseable
the run is measuring output formatting and is reported as that -- the same
condition that invalidated a run in ai-compliance-checker and forced a re-run
after a token cap was raised. The cap here is 4000 from the start.

WHAT WOULD INVALIDATE THE STRATIFICATION. If the ANNOUNCED stratum is answered
correctly at a rate no better than the SILENT stratum, the models are not
reading the health output at all and the three strata are not measuring three
different things.
